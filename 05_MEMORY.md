# AI Masterpiece — Memory & Second Mind

## Core Principle
> Remember everything ≠ surface everything.

The system requires a persistent second mind with selective retrieval rather than dumping complete history into model context.

## Layers
- **Raw Archive:** preserve authorised historical conversations, research, sources, decisions, memory changes, audit history and historical project states.
- **Active Knowledge:** current useful knowledge that may be reorganised, deprioritised or updated without destroying history.
- **Personal Memory:** facts/preferences/context about the user.
- **General Knowledge:** research and concepts, clearly distinguished from personal memory.
- **Relationships:** links between entities, projects, concepts, decisions and research.
- **Inbox:** uncertain or semantically important items awaiting review.
- **Retrieval:** relevance + trust + recency + importance + current context.

## Authority
User retains ultimate authority over persistent memory. Confirmed knowledge may be organised automatically. User corrections outrank AI assumptions.

## Provenance
Important knowledge should retain source/evidence, date, confidence, related decisions/research/experiments and history where practical. Trivial memories do not require forensic provenance.

## Confidence
Distinguish confirmed, supported, uncertain and inferred information. High importance does not mean high confidence.

## Freshness
Track freshness selectively for information likely to change. Stale does not mean false; historical information remains available.

## Changes
Never silently rewrite history. Preserve old values/conclusions, record new values, dates, reasons and evidence.

## Forgetting
Normal retrieval excludes forgotten information. Historical/audit retention may remain unless explicit hard deletion is required.

## Consolidation
Low-risk organisation is autonomous: duplicate merging, links, metadata, navigation and structural cleanup. Meaning is protected: changes to factual claims, confidence, interpretation, important relationships or conclusions go to review.

## Research
Research findings should preserve sources, evidence, confidence and date so future decisions can be traced back to why they were made.
