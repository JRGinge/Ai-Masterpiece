# AI Masterpiece — Automation

## Status
Future capability. Not enabled in the initial build.

## Principle
Automation may operate only inside explicitly authorised scope and can never bypass security permissions.

## Candidate Uses
- Scheduled research
- Memory maintenance
- Monitoring
- Notifications
- Repetitive project tasks
- Reports
- Future integrations

## Autonomous Research
Deep research may run for extended periods when explicitly authorised. Autonomous research does not imply autonomous machine control.

## Failure Behaviour
Expected minor failures may be safely recovered where appropriate. Unclear or consequential failures require investigation and/or user involvement. Dangerous/destructive situations stop. Failures must never be hidden or reported as success.

## Maintenance
Scheduled maintenance may organise and verify information but must not silently perform consequential external actions.
