# Research Archive

This directory stores curated research extracted from project conversations and external research.

## Processing rule

Research is converted into structured knowledge rather than copied as raw chat transcripts.

For each research source, capture where supported:

- research findings
- evidence / sources
- decisions made
- alternatives considered or rejected
- requirements and constraints
- unresolved questions
- contradictions with existing project knowledge

## Knowledge states

- Confirmed
- Research finding
- Provisional
- Open
- Rejected
- Conflict

## Source integrity

A research item is only marked complete when the underlying source has actually been reviewed. A consolidated project master or summary must not be represented as a complete transcript of the originating chat.
