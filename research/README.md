# Research Archive

This directory stores curated research extracted from project conversations and external research.

## Processing rule

Research is converted into structured knowledge rather than copied as an unstructured chat dump.

For each research source, capture where supported:

- research findings
- evidence / sources
- decisions made
- alternatives considered or rejected
- requirements and constraints
- unresolved questions
- contradictions with existing project knowledge
- confidence / uncertainty
- conditions for re-verification

## Knowledge states

- Confirmed
- Research finding
- Provisional
- Open
- Rejected
- Conflict
- Historical / Superseded

## Source integrity

The uploaded `LOCAL_AI_PROJECT_MASTER.md` and compiled project research files are treated as project source material for this Second Mind build. We must preserve the distinction between a compiled project record and an individual raw chat transcript; neither should be described as a full transcript unless it actually is one.

## Research-to-knowledge flow

```text
SOURCE
  ↓
EXTRACT
  ↓
CLASSIFY
  ↓
CROSS-CHECK
  ↓
DECISION / FINDING
  ↓
CANONICAL DOCUMENT
  ↓
SECOND MIND
```
